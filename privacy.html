Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fyb video-publishing automation project (“Fyb”, “we”, or “the project”) handles information when its owner operates the workflow to prepare and upload videos to a YouTube channel.

Information processed

Depending on a workflow run, Fyb may process:

  • Google OAuth authorization credentials needed to access the YouTube upload function. These credentials are configured by the project owner as repository automation secrets.
  • Video and publishing information supplied to or generated by the workflow, such as video files, titles, descriptions, and related metadata.
  • Technical execution information, such as workflow status and error logs, made available by the automation platform.

Fyb requests the YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). It is used to upload content to the YouTube channel selected and authorized by the account owner.

How information is used

Information is used to run the requested video preparation and YouTube publishing workflow, diagnose failures, and maintain the project. Fyb does not sell personal information.

Services operated by third parties

The workflow relies on third-party services, including Google/YouTube for account authorization and video publishing, and GitHub Actions and GitHub repository features for source-code hosting and workflow execution. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Fyb does not control their independent practices.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • YouTube Terms of Service

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials configured by the project owner are intended to be stored in GitHub repository or environment secrets, not in public website pages. Workflow logs and artifacts may be retained by GitHub according to the repository and account settings. The project owner can remove configured secrets and delete workflow artifacts or repository data where the relevant service permits.

Security and account controls

Access to the YouTube channel is based on Google authorization. The account owner can review or revoke Fyb’s access in their Google Account third-party connections. If access is revoked, uploads requiring that authorization will stop until the owner authorizes the workflow again. No secret tokens or client secrets should be shared publicly.

Children

Fyb is an automation tool and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the workflow or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
