Fyb is a small automation project designed to prepare video content and publish it to a YouTube channel authorized by its owner.

What Fyb does

Fyb runs an owner-controlled workflow. When enabled, it can use YouTube authorization granted by the channel owner to upload video content and its associated publishing information.

Fyb is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
